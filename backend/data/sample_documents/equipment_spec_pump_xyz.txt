IDENTIFICATION
Equipment ID: PUMP-XYZ-2019
Manufacturer: Grundfos A/S (Denmark)
Model Number: SPM 150-11 / 4.0 kW-90
Serial Number: DK-RP-20190515-012847
Installation Date: 2019-08-22
Application: Coolant circulation system (closed-loop), Steel plant Unit A

PERFORMANCE SPECIFICATIONS
Rated Flow Rate: 150 L/min (at 1450 RPM)
Rated Pressure: 25 bar (362 PSI)
Maximum Allowable Pressure: 32 bar (464 PSI) - relief valve setting
Suction Head (Max): -8.0 m (cavitation threshold: <1 bar inlet pressure)
Discharge Head: 250 m (theoretical maximum)
Power Rating: 4.0 kW electric motor (1450 RPM, 3-phase, 50 Hz, 6.5 A)
Efficiency (Overall): 75% at rated conditions
Noise Level: 76 dB(A) at 1 meter

OPERATIONAL LIMITS
Operating Temperature Range: 5°C to 60°C (ambient), fluid max 65°C
Fluid Temperature (Design): 40-55°C (optimal range)
Inlet Temperature Limit: 70°C (above this, NBR seals at risk)
Ambient Temperature: -10°C to +50°C
Storage Temperature: -20°C to +70°C
Relative Humidity: 0-95% (non-condensing)
Altitude: Max 1000 m above sea level

CONSTRUCTION DETAILS
Pump Type: Centrifugal, vertical turbine, single-stage impeller
Impeller Design: Closed radial vanes, 150 mm diameter
Housing Material: Ductile iron (EN-GJS-500-07)
Impeller Material: Nickel-aluminum bronze (CuNiAl)
Shaft Material: Stainless steel AISI 316L (corrosion-resistant)
Shaft Diameter: 20 mm, hardened to 50-52 HRC
Bearing Type: Deep-groove ball bearings (SKF 6205-2RS)
Bearing Lubrication: Packed grease (Mobil SHC 629)

SEAL SYSTEM SPECIFICATION
Primary Seal Type: Mechanical seal - Cartridge design
Primary Seal Material: NBR (nitrile rubber) Type A, carbon-ceramic face
Secondary Seal Type: Mechanical seal - Cartridge design
Secondary Seal Material: FKM (Viton fluoroelastomer) Type B, tungsten carbide face
Seal Spring: Stainless steel spring cartridge, Belleville type
Seal Cavity: Pressure-balanced, allowing operation up to 25 bar
Leakage Rate: <0.5 mL/day (acceptable per ISO 4414)
OEM Seal Kit Part Numbers:
  - Primary (NBR): Grundfos 96087211
  - Secondary (FKM): Grundfos 96087414
  - Spring assembly: Grundfos 96087098
Seal Service Life: 24-36 months (design), actual 20-24 months under steel plant conditions

PIPING CONNECTIONS
Inlet Connection: Flanged, DN 50 (2 inches), PN 25 bar, ISO 4401 (NG10)
  - Inlet Piping Size: DN 50 with 40-micron strainer (Hydac RFBN/HC110)
  - Recommended Inlet Velocity: 0.6-1.2 m/s
Outlet Connection: Flanged, DN 40 (1.5 inches), PN 25 bar, ISO 4401 (NG10)
  - Outlet Piping Size: DN 40
  - Recommended Outlet Velocity: 1.5-2.5 m/s
Drain Connection: DN 20 (0.75 inches), gravity return to reservoir
Flushing Port: ISO 4401 cavity (NG10), for system cleaning

HYDRAULIC FLUID SPECIFICATIONS
Fluid Type: ISO VG 32 hydraulic oil (mineral or synthetic)
Recommended Brands: Mobil DTE 10 Excel 32, Shell Tellus S2 V 32
Viscosity Index: Min 95 (temperature stability)
Cleanliness Level: ISO 4406 16/14/11 or better (target 16/14/11)
Maximum Water Content: 300 ppm (moisture degradation threshold)
Acid Number: Max 0.5 mg KOH/g (oxidation stability)
Bulk Modulus: 1.65 GPa (typical)
Tank Capacity: 250 liters minimum (recommended circulation ratio 3:1)
Change Interval: 2000 operating hours or annually, whichever comes first

MATERIALS & CORROSION RESISTANCE
Housing: Ductile iron, painted with epoxy primer (80 microns) + polyurethane topcoat (60 microns)
Fasteners: Stainless steel A4 (AISI 316L) bolts, minimum grade 8.8
Seals: FKM (Viton) for temperature and chemical resistance
Shaft: Passivated stainless steel AISI 316L
Bearing Cage: Brass (self-lubricating in grease)
Corrosion Class: C3 (steel plant environment - high risk)

CERTIFICATIONS & COMPLIANCE
ISO 4414: Hydraulic safety standard (pressure system safety, hose sizing, relief valve settings)
EN 12922: Hydraulic fluid classification and specification
ISO 13849-1: Functional safety of hydraulic systems (Category 3, PLd)

ASME B31.1: Code for pressure piping

CE Marking: Applied 2019-06-10 (compliance with PED and machinery directive)

Insurance: Covered under AXA Industrial Liability Policy (max 5 bar design pressure recognition)
